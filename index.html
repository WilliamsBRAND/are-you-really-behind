Maybe you’re not behind. Maybe you’re just measuring your life with someone else’s clock.

In a world where everyone seems to be moving faster, achieving earlier and constantly announcing their next big win, it is easy to wonder if you’re falling behind.

But are you really behind?

This is a high-level conversation about the timelines we create for ourselves, the pressure to keep up, finding purpose in our own journey and understanding what progress actually looks like.

We are talking about pace, purpose, progress and pressure.
